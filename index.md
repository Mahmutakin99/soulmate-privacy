# SoulMate Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 23, 2026

## Overview
SoulMate is a private messaging app for couples. We take your privacy seriously.

## Data We Collect
- **Account data:** Email address and name (required for sign-in)
- **Messages:** End-to-end encrypted; stored on Firebase; only you and your partner can read them
- **Profile photo:** Encrypted and transferred only to your paired partner
- **Location:** Optional, only shared with your partner when enabled; never stored on servers
- **Mood status:** Optional, shared only with your partner

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not use your data for advertising
- We do not store decrypted message content

## Third-Party Services
- **Firebase (Google):** Authentication, encrypted data storage — [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## Data Deletion
Delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete Account) to permanently erase all your data.
